toned by `scripts/build-sounds.mjs` (the recipe lists the exact stretch of
each recording every sound is made of). Every recording is free to use in a
commercial game: CC0 and public-domain works need no credit, and the CC BY
works are credited here as their licences ask. The US Army bugle calls are
works of the US government, in the public domain.
