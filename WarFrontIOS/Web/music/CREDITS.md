# Music credits

The music WarFront plays under its menus and its campaign. Every track is free for a commercial game; each is credited
here as its licence asks. Built by `node scripts/build-music.mjs` (never edit the .ogg files by hand).

- Source: https://www.scottbuckley.com.au/library/shadows-and-dust/
- Licence: CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- Credit: 'Shadows and Dust' by Scott Buckley, released under CC-BY 4.0. www.scottbuckley.com.au
